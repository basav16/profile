How it runs

Every source the recruiting team already uses feeds one Claude agent. It decides who needs a reply, writes it, sends the routine ones itself, and posts everything to this page for the team.

1 · Data sources

LeverCandidates, stages, interviewers
Recruiting inboxGmail threads and replies
AI outreach toolWho was contacted, who answered
Google CalendarInterviews held and booked
Interview scorecardsRatings from Lever feedback forms or a Google Sheet; notes from AI note takers like Granola or Otter

2 · Connect

Connectors (MCP)Lever API, Gmail, Calendar, Google Sheets, note taker, read-only by default
Daily syncRuns every morning and every 2 hours
One candidate recordLast touch, stage, owner, notes in one row

3 · Claude agent

Check the clockCompare each wait to its stage window
Score fitRate 0 to 100 from resume, scorecards and notes
Write the follow-upIn the company's voice, right for the stage
Send or holdRoutine: sends itself. Final round, CEO-owned or a candidate question: holds for a person

4 · Outputs

Email to the candidateSent from the recruiting inbox
Lever updatedNote and activity logged on the profile
This dashboardHosted link the hiring team opens
Weekly scorecardOn-time rate and drop-off, to the CEO
Week 1Connect the sourcesRead access to Lever, the recruiting inbox and calendars. Map stages and owners.
Week 1Build the agentResponse windows, scoring rubric, email templates in the company's voice, send-or-hold rules.
Week 2Run in review modeEvery draft goes to a person first. Tune tone and rules on real candidates.
Week 3Turn on auto-reply and hostRoutine replies go out on their own. The dashboard is a hosted link, shared with anyone who has access.
