About

8 years in the room where decisions get made.

I started as an analyst at a pilot training company in Delhi and became the person its founders called before big decisions: what to invest in, what to walk away from, and how to fund growth. Since then I've advised founders through my own firm, helped a $150B company's CEO and CFO decide on an acquisition at UBS, finished my MBA at Columbia, and now report to the CEO of a Khosla-backed consumer brand. The pattern is the same: get clear on the decision, bring the numbers that settle it, then stay to make it happen. Finance is my depth; working across ops, GTM and AI makes me a useful generalist next to the people running the business.
