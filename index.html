Basav SharmaFinance & Special Operations Lead at Because · San Francisco

For 8 years, I've worked next to founders and CEOs. My focus has never been an industry. It's always been the mission: the founders, and the problems they're solving.

I started in aviation, helping grow a pilot training organization into India's number one. I bought aircraft, opened air bases, ran a stake sale, and rolled out new software and processes. Ironically, I never trained to be a pilot.

Since then, I founded Stratrun to help small businesses around the world access consulting. I cleared CFA Level II, earned my MBA at Columbia, and took a deep dive into American industrial companies in investment banking at UBS. Today, I use AI to automate finance and special operations at a senior care company.

Every step has been a mission: making Chimes India's #1 flight training organization, bringing the sharpest insight and execution to deals that maximize shareholder value, and now getting seniors the best-quality adult diapers. That one makes me proud, because I took care of my grandfather when I was in Delhi, and this mission reminds me how I can make the lives of seniors better.

Taking on a mission gives me agency, in the business and across its industry. I do my best work where the stakes are high and the company is scaling fast.

What I love is sitting with a founder, framing the problem, shaping the strategy and solving it. After working across industries, and with AI changing how every business runs, I want to work with founders who are disrupting their industry.

Who am I?

I'm the Swiss Army knife in the room, and I've always known it. I'm the one tool a founder reaches for in a tight spot, so the team skips the trip to the tool shed and keeps moving fast.
